# Design direction

Photo is a darkroom contact sheet for a small visual archive. A verified frame
is shown at proof scale, while absent imagery is stated plainly instead of
being replaced by a fake photograph.

- Palette: graphite room, warm paper, olive chemistry marks, and acid-lime
  registration accents.
- Type: Fraunces carries the editorial caption voice; Figtree handles controls
  and archive metadata.
- Form: proof sheet, archive rail, registration lines, and an authored skyline
  drawing explicitly labelled as a proof illustration.
- Content truth: the repository currently contains one metadata item and no
  attached image asset; the Work shelf is intentionally empty.
- Responsive rule: the proof and caption become one vertical reading path on
  narrow screens.
